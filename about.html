Meet Charles Parks

Over 20 years of handcraftsmanship.

A craftsman’s approach

Charles Parks has spent more than 20 years working with his hands. Through Charlie’s Chairs he reupholsters and restores furniture, builds custom pieces, and sells upholstery fabric.

His approach is simple: respect the piece and its story, be honest about what’s possible, and do the work properly, from the frame to the final detail.

He also shares the craft on YouTube, so you can see how the work gets done.

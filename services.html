Upholstery & reupholstery

New fabric on a good frame

Many pieces have solid bones under tired fabric. Reupholstery replaces the worn covering, and where needed the padding beneath it, so a chair, sofa, bench, ottoman or headboard looks and feels right again.

  • Chairs, sofas, loveseats, sectionals and ottomans
  • Dining chairs and benches
  • Headboards
  • Fabric guidance for how the piece will be used

Restoration

From the frame to the final detail

Restoration starts with an honest look at the frame, springs and structure. Not every piece can be saved, and Charles will tell you plainly. When it can, the goal is a piece you’ll keep using for years.

Close-up photos of damaged areas help Charles assess a restoration before you talk.

Privacy notice

When you send a quote request or message, we collect the details you enter (such as your name, email, phone number and a description of your project) and any photos you upload.

We use this information only to review your request and to contact you about it. Photos you upload are stored privately and are not posted on this website or shared publicly. Only Charlie’s Chairs can view them.

Your information is stored with our website provider (Supabase). We don’t sell your information. To ask us to delete your request and photos, email or call us using the details below.
